## Why StillTabs

**We're on your side.** Most apps, including many that promise to fix your screen time, are built to take as much as they can: your time, your data, a subscription you forget to cancel. StillTabs asks for nothing. [Why we built it](why-we-built-stilltabs.md) · [What's wrong with Reels blockers](guides/whats-wrong-with-reels-blocker-apps.md)


1. **It removes the problem, not the app.** Reels, Shorts, Explore, suggested posts and autoplay go; DMs, stories, search, subscriptions and friends' posts stay. App blockers and Screen Time can only lock the whole app, messages included.
2. **It's actually free.** No Pro tier, no trial, no ads, nothing to buy. Most Reels blockers are free to download and then sell a subscription.
3. **Your habit still works.** StillTabs adds Home Screen icons named Instagram, YouTube, Facebook, X and Reddit. Tap where you always tap and land in a version without Reels, with no Screen Time permission needed. [How it works](guides/instagram-icon-without-reels.md)
4. **It feels like the real app.** Instagram's toolbar is drawn natively, every tab stays loaded so switching is instant, and you get around with edge swipes instead of a floating button. [What it looks like](guides/does-stilltabs-feel-like-instagram.md)
5. **You choose how strict.** Four modes: Off, Standard, Strict and Blackout (only search, profiles and DMs). Switch any time.
6. **Nothing on your phone gets locked or watched.** No Screen Time access, no screen broadcast, no device management, no Safari extension permissions.
7. **Private by default.** "Data Not Linked to You" on the App Store; analytics are off unless you turn them on; pages load straight from each platform with no proxy, so passwords and messages never pass through StillTabs. Even [stilltabs.com loads zero trackers](compare/reels-blocker-website-trackers.md).
8. **Five platforms, five accounts each.** Instagram, YouTube, Facebook, X and Reddit, with up to five sign-ins per platform.

**Honest limits:** iPhone only (no Android yet), no TikTok or Snapchat, and Facebook, X and Reddit are still in beta.

## FAQ

**Is StillTabs really free?**
Yes. No subscription, no trial, no Pro version, no ads. The App Store shows no in-app purchases.

**Does StillTabs keep Instagram DMs?**
Yes. Messages, stories, search and posts from people you follow stay. Blackout mode leaves only search, profiles and DMs.

**Does it change the real Instagram app?**
No. iOS doesn't let one app change another. StillTabs shows Instagram inside its own app, and its Home Screen icon named Instagram means your thumb goes to the same place as before.

**Can StillTabs see my password or messages?**
No. You sign in on each platform's own page, pages load straight from the platform, and StillTabs runs no server in between.

**Is there an Android version or TikTok support?**
Not yet. StillTabs is iPhone only and doesn't support TikTok or Snapchat.
