# Short-form video by the numbers

*Updated 4 November 2026*

**Short-form video is the fastest-growing part of social media: YouTube Shorts averages over 200 billion daily views, Meta says Instagram video time rose more than 30% in a year, and 36% of US teens say they're on at least one major platform almost constantly.** StillTabs answers with 43 separate filter rules across five platforms.

## What these numbers mean

**Short-form video is where the platforms' growth and revenue are, so it's where their design effort goes.** A Reel or a Short is short enough that stopping never feels necessary, and the next one is already loaded. YouTube counts a Shorts view every time one starts or replays, which is how a single format reaches 200 billion views a day.

That's why turning short-form video off is hard to do from inside the apps: Instagram has no switch for Reels, and YouTube's Shorts limit can be dismissed by adults. See [what each platform allows](turn-off-short-form-video.md).
