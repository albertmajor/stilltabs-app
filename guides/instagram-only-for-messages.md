# How to use Instagram only for messages

*Updated 4 November 2026*

**Instagram has no messages-only mode, but StillTabs' Blackout mode is one.** It removes the feed, Reels, stories and Explore and leaves just your DMs, search and profiles. It's free on iPhone, and you can switch back whenever you like.

## Why there's no DM-only Instagram

Instagram's messages live inside the same app as its feed and Reels, and its settings only shape what you're recommended. Quiet mode mutes notifications; it doesn't hide anything. So opening Instagram for one message always starts in the feed.

## How to set it up

1. Install [StillTabs](https://apps.apple.com/app/id6761367777) (free) and open the Instagram tile.

2. Sign in on Instagram's own page.

3. Go to Settings → Blocking Mode and choose **Blackout**.

**You'll see search, profiles and messages, and nothing else.** If you'd like to keep stories and friends' posts, choose Strict or Standard instead.

## Modes compared

- **Off**: the platform stays close to normal; only the "open in app" prompts and pop-ups go.

- **Standard**: Shorts, the Reels tab, Explore, "For you" tabs, suggested posts and autoplay go. Your feeds stay.

- **Strict**: short-form video is gone everywhere and home feeds give way to search. Instagram keeps your stories; YouTube opens on your subscriptions.

- **Blackout**: no feeds, no stories, no Explore. Just search, profiles and your messages.
