# How to remove Reels from Facebook

*Updated 4 November 2026*

**Facebook has no setting to turn off Reels; hiding a reel or choosing "show less" only lowers similar ones for a while.** StillTabs, which is free, hides Facebook's Reels tab and keeps friends, groups and messages. Facebook support is in beta.

## What Facebook's own settings do

- **Hide reel, Interested and Not interested** change which reels Facebook ranks for you.

- **Your algorithm** (October 2026) lets you manage topics for Feed and Reels.

- **The Feeds and Friends tabs** show posts from friends, groups and Pages, but the main feed still mixes in Reels.

- **Snooze** hides a person or Page for 30 days.

## Facebook without the Reels tab, step by step

1. Install [StillTabs](https://apps.apple.com/app/id6761367777) and open the Facebook tile.

2. Sign in on Facebook's own page.

3. In **Standard** mode, the Reels tab is hidden.

**Friends, groups and messages stay.** Facebook support is in beta: it works best with Facebook set to English, and Reels mixed into the main feed can still appear. We're improving it in updates; tell us what slips through at [support@stilltabs.com](mailto:support@stilltabs.com).
