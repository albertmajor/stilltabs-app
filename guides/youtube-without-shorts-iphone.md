# How to use YouTube without Shorts on iPhone

*Updated 8 November 2026*

**You can't switch Shorts off inside the official YouTube app; to use YouTube on iPhone without Shorts, open it in an app or Safari extension that removes them, such as StillTabs, Nope or Slowth, all free to download.** YouTube's own Shorts feed limit can be set to zero, but it's a reminder you can dismiss, and "Show fewer Shorts" only lowers how often they appear. StillTabs removes the Shorts tab and the Shorts shelves on Home in its Standard mode.

## What YouTube itself lets you do

**There is no setting that removes Shorts from the YouTube app.** There are two settings that make them less prominent.

- **Shorts feed limit.** Sign in, then tap You → Settings → Time management → Shorts feed limit. YouTube's help page says you can pick from a list of limits, including zero. When you reach the limit, a message reminds you, and you can dismiss or ignore it. News coverage of the zero setting reports that the Shorts shelf on the Home page disappears, and that you still get the prompt when you watch the first Short of a session.

- **Show fewer Shorts.** On Home, scroll to a grid of Shorts, tap More at the top of the grid, then Show fewer Shorts. YouTube says this means fewer recommended Shorts on pages such as Home and Watch Next. It doesn't remove them, and you can undo it in My Activity.

Both are worth switching on if you want to stay in the official app. Both can be overridden: the limit is a reminder you can dismiss, and Show fewer Shorts only lowers how often they are recommended.

## Option 1: a separate app (StillTabs)

StillTabs is a free iPhone app that shows YouTube and Instagram inside the app with Shorts, Reels and autoplay removed. You keep search, your subscriptions and the videos you came for.

1. Install StillTabs from the App Store and open the YouTube tile. It works without signing in; sign in on Google's own page if you want your subscriptions.

2. Pick a mode: Off, Standard, Strict or Blackout, all free. **Standard** already removes the Shorts tab and the Shorts shelves on Home. **Strict** also removes Shorts from search and channels and opens YouTube on your subscriptions. **Blackout** leaves only search, profiles and messages.

3. Optional: add a Home Screen icon named YouTube by visiting stilltabs.com/go/youtube in Safari. It opens StillTabs, so your thumb goes where it always did. [How the icons work](instagram-icon-without-reels.md).

There is no prompt to dismiss: Shorts stay gone until you change the mode yourself. StillTabs never locks or changes the YouTube app, and asks for no Screen Time access, no screen broadcast and no Safari extension. Pages load straight from YouTube, so your password never passes through StillTabs. More on Subscriptions-only viewing in [YouTube with subscriptions only](youtube-subscriptions-only.md).

## Option 2: YouTube in Safari with an extension (Nope, Slowth)

If you're happy to watch YouTube on youtube.com in Safari, a Safari extension can hide Shorts there. You turn it on in Settings → Apps → Safari → Extensions and allow it for youtube.com. It doesn't change the YouTube app.

- **Nope** is free with no in-app purchases. Its App Store page lists toggles to hide Shorts everywhere on the site (sidebar, Home shelf, search chip, channel pages), redirect Shorts links to Home, turn off autoplay and previews, and hide the Home feed so YouTube opens on Subscriptions. Privacy label: Data Not Collected. Needs iOS 17.0.

- **Slowth** is free, with in-app purchases from $0.99 to $8.99 (Cup of Coffee, Coffee Beans, Climbing Pass). It hides YouTube Shorts, Instagram and Facebook Reels and X's Explore and trends in Safari, can block TikTok, and has a 24-hour strict lock. It also has an optional mode on iPhone and iPad that uses iOS screen recording to spot Shorts (and Instagram Reels and Stories) in the official apps, and then blocks the whole app with Screen Time. Privacy label: Data Not Collected. Needs iOS 16.0. It's open source.

## Common questions

### Can you remove Shorts from the YouTube app on iPhone?

Not completely. The YouTube app has no setting to remove Shorts or the Shorts tab. The Shorts feed limit can be set to zero, but it is a reminder you can dismiss. To remove Shorts, use YouTube through an app such as StillTabs or a Safari extension such as Nope or Slowth.

### Does setting the Shorts feed limit to zero remove Shorts?

No. YouTube's help page says a message appears when you reach the limit and you can dismiss or ignore it. News reports say the Shorts shelf on Home disappears at zero, but you can still watch Shorts after a prompt you can override.

### Is there a YouTube app without Shorts for iPhone?

StillTabs shows YouTube inside its own app with the Shorts tab and the Shorts shelves on Home removed in Standard mode, and autoplay removed. It is free, with no in-app purchases, no ads and no account needed. It is new, with no ratings yet.

### Do Safari extensions remove Shorts from the YouTube app?

No. Nope and Slowth hide Shorts on youtube.com in Safari. Slowth has a separate mode that uses screen recording to spot Shorts in the official app, and then blocks the whole app with Screen Time.

### Can I keep my YouTube subscriptions without Shorts?

Yes. Sign in on Google's own page inside StillTabs and your subscriptions stay, without the Shorts tab. Nope can also open youtube.com on Subscriptions in Safari.
