# How to stop YouTube autoplay on iPhone

*Updated 4 November 2026*

**Turn off autoplay in YouTube under your profile picture → Settings → Autoplay, and stop feed previews under Settings → General → Playback in feeds → Off.** That stops the next video starting by itself, but Shorts and the endless homepage stay. StillTabs removes those too, for free.

## YouTube's own settings

1. **Autoplay next video:** tap your profile picture → Settings → Autoplay, and switch it off.

2. **Previews in your feed:** Settings → General → Playback in feeds → Off (or Wi-Fi only).

## What those settings leave

Shorts still have their own tab and shelves, the Shorts player still scrolls endlessly, and Home is still an endless list of recommendations. **StillTabs' Standard mode stops previews and removes the Shorts tab and shelves; Strict also removes Shorts everywhere and opens YouTube on your subscriptions.**
