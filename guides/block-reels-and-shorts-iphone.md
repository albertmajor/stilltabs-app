# How to block Reels and Shorts on iPhone, for free

*Updated 4 November 2026*

**To block Reels and Shorts on iPhone for free, install StillTabs and open Instagram, YouTube or Facebook from it.** Standard mode removes Reels, Shorts, Explore and autoplay inside each platform and keeps your messages, search and the people you follow. No subscription, no account.

## Step by step

1. **Install StillTabs** from the [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/app/id6761367777). It's free, with no in-app purchases. It needs iOS 16 or later.

2. **Pick your platforms** on the launcher: Instagram, YouTube, Facebook, X and Reddit.

3. **Sign in** on each platform's own login page. StillTabs has no account of its own and never sees your password.

4. **Choose a mode** in Settings → Blocking Mode. Standard removes the Reels and Shorts tabs, Explore, "For you" tabs, suggested posts and autoplay. Strict removes short-form video everywhere.

5. **Add the widget** to your Home Screen if you like, so StillTabs is what you reach for instead of the native apps.

## Why not just block the apps?

Blocking Instagram also blocks your messages, and blocking YouTube blocks the tutorial you actually needed. **Most people don't want less Instagram; they want no Reels.** Removing just the addictive parts means you don't have to choose.
