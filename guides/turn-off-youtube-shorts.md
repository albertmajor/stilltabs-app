# Can you turn off YouTube Shorts?

*Updated 4 November 2026*

**Partly. YouTube's Shorts feed limit can be set to zero, which hides the Shorts shelf on Home, but for adults it's a limit you can dismiss, and Shorts still play from links and elsewhere in the app.** To remove Shorts everywhere in YouTube on iPhone and keep search and subscriptions, use StillTabs, which is free.

## YouTube's Shorts feed limit

**YouTube added a Shorts feed limit in 2026**: You → Settings → Time management → Shorts feed limit. You can set it as low as zero. With zero, the Shorts shelf disappears from Home.

It has two limits of its own. **For adults it's soft**: when you reach it, YouTube offers to dismiss or ignore it. And it limits the Shorts feed, not Shorts themselves: they still play when someone sends you one, and reports say they still show up in places like Subscriptions. For supervised teen accounts, a limit set by a parent can't be dismissed.

## Other YouTube settings that help

- **Show fewer Shorts**: tap ⋯ on the Shorts grid on Home. This lowers how often they appear; it doesn't remove them.

- **Not interested** and **Don't recommend channel** work on Shorts too.

- **Delete and turn off watch history**: with no history, YouTube removes recommendations from Home. Pausing alone isn't enough if you already have a long history, and you lose your history in the process.

## How to remove YouTube Shorts everywhere on iPhone

1. Install [StillTabs](https://apps.apple.com/app/id6761367777) (free) and open the YouTube tile. YouTube works without signing in; sign in on Google's page if you want your subscriptions.

2. In **Standard** mode, the Shorts tab and the Shorts shelves on Home are gone, the Shorts player can't be scrolled endlessly, and videos stop playing previews by themselves.

3. In **Strict** mode, Shorts are gone everywhere, in search and on channel pages too; Shorts links open the homepage instead, and YouTube opens straight on your subscriptions.

**Search, your subscriptions and the video you came for stay.** Unlike the Shorts feed limit, there's no prompt to dismiss: Shorts stay gone until you change the mode yourself.
