## Why StillTabs can be free

**StillTabs runs entirely on your phone, so there is no server bill per user to pass on.** The filters are rules that hide parts of each platform's page as it loads; pages come straight from Instagram, YouTube and the rest. Nothing about it needs a subscription to keep running, and there are no ads either.

Free also means nothing to cancel: no renewal date, no refund request, no trial that turns into a charge.

## What you get for free

- Instagram without Reels, Explore and suggested posts, opening on the Following feed

- YouTube without Shorts, autoplaying previews and the endless homepage
