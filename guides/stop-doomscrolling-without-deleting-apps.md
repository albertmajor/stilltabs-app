# How to stop doomscrolling without deleting your apps

*Updated 4 November 2026*

**The most reliable way to stop doomscrolling without deleting your apps is to remove the parts built for it: short-form video, endless feeds and autoplay.** Limits and blockers ask for willpower each time; removing Reels and Shorts means there's nothing to scroll. StillTabs does this for free on iPhone.

## Why limits and deleting rarely last

Deleting Instagram also deletes your messages and friends, so it usually comes back. Screen-time limits can be ignored with a tap. Pauses before opening an app help with habit, but once you're in, the feed is the same. **All of them fight the design instead of changing it.**

## What actually helps

1. **Remove short-form video.** Reels and Shorts are the fastest loop; take them out and most of the pull goes with them.

2. **End the infinite feed.** StillTabs' Strict mode replaces home feeds with search, so you go looking for something instead of being shown everything.

3. **Turn off autoplay.** Videos that start by themselves decide what you watch next.

4. **See your time.** StillTabs' tiles grow with the time you spend on each platform, so you notice without being nagged.

5. **Keep what you came for.** Messages, friends, subscriptions and search stay, so there's no reason to go back to the full app.

## Set it up in two minutes

Install [StillTabs](https://apps.apple.com/app/id6761367777), open Instagram or YouTube from the launcher, sign in on the platform's own page and pick Standard or Strict. It's free, with no account.
