# 9 checks before you trust a Reels blocker or screen-time app

*Updated 4 November 2026*

**Before you install a Reels blocker, check four things on its App Store page (the in-app purchase list, the privacy label, who makes it, the ratings) four in its terms (refunds, renewal, arbitration, what you must hand over), and one on its website: what loads before you agree to anything.** Each takes under a minute. StillTabs passes all nine: it's free, has no tracking label, needs no account, and its website loads no trackers.

## The nine checks

| Check | Where to look | Red flag | StillTabs |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1. What it really costs | App Store → In-App Purchases | Weekly plans; a long list of prices | No in-app purchases |
| 2. Tracking | App Store → App Privacy | "Data Used to Track You" | None; "Data Not Linked to You" |
| 3. What is linked to you | App Store → App Privacy | Contacts, location, browsing history linked to you | Nothing linked to you |
| 4. What you must hand over | Sign-up screen, privacy policy | Phone number, an account, Screen Time or device management | No account, no permissions over other apps |
| 5. Refunds | Terms | "We generally do not provide refunds" | Nothing to refund |
| 6. Renewal and price changes | Terms | Prices that differ per user; terms that change "without notice" | No subscription |
| 7. Disputes | Terms | Binding arbitration, class-action waiver | Nothing to dispute |
| 8. Does it do what you want | Listing, help pages | Blocks the whole app when you only wanted Reels gone | Removes Reels and Shorts, keeps DMs |
| 9. What its website loads | Open its website (or use a tracker blocker that counts them) | Advertising pixels (Meta, Google Ads, Snapchat) before you agree to anything | None: no third-party requests at all |

*Red-flag examples are quoted from apps' own terms and listings in the sources below, checked 4 November 2026.*

## The red flags, from real terms

- **No refunds:** SocialLite's terms say "We generally do not provide refunds"; Opal says it is "unable to provide refunds" for App Store purchases.

- **Weekly billing:** Opal lists weekly plans from $4.99; Stillfeed's $2.99 a week adds up to about $155 a year.

- **Different prices for different people:** Stillfeed "may show different subscription prices to different users".

- **Terms that move:** Freedom's terms "may be updated by us from time to time without notice".

- **Your phone number:** SocialLite requires one to sign up.

- **Tracking:** 7 of the 14 apps we checked carry Apple's "Data Used to Track You" label.

- **A banner that isn't there:** SocialLite's cookie banner says "Nothing is tracked unless you accept", but its code has had the banner switched off since 7 October 2026 while Meta, Google Ads and Snapchat pixels load ([details](../compare/reels-blocker-website-trackers.md)).
