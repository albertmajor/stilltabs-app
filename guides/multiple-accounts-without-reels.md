# Several Instagram or YouTube accounts, without Reels or Shorts

*Updated 4 November 2026*

**StillTabs keeps up to five accounts per platform, each signed in separately, and removes Reels, Shorts and Explore from all of them.** You switch accounts from the platform's tile; nothing is shared between them, and there's no StillTabs account. It's free.

## How it works

1. Open a platform from the StillTabs launcher and sign in on its own page.

2. Add another account for that platform, up to five.

3. Each account keeps its own sign-in; the blocking mode you set applies to all of them.

## Who uses it

People with a personal and a work Instagram, creators who check a brand account, and anyone who shares a YouTube account at home. Separate sign-ins mean you never post from the wrong account, and none of them shows you Reels.
