# How to get rid of the "For you" tab on X

*Updated 4 November 2026*

**X doesn't let you remove the "For you" tab or make Following the default.** To use X on iPhone without "For you", suggested accounts and trends, use StillTabs (free); X support is in beta.

## What X allows

X lets you switch between "For you" and "Following", and pin Lists. That's it: there's no setting to hide "For you", and in 2026 the Following tab itself started defaulting to "Popular" rather than newest first.

## X without "For you"

1. Install [StillTabs](https://apps.apple.com/app/id6761367777) and open the X tile.

2. Sign in on X's own page.

3. In **Standard** mode, the "For you" tab, suggested accounts and trends are gone. Following, search and the news stay.

X support is in beta: X changes its pages often, and when something slips through we fix it in an update. Tell us at [support@stilltabs.com](mailto:support@stilltabs.com).
