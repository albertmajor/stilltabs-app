# Can you turn off Instagram Reels?

*Updated 4 November 2026*

**No, Instagram has no setting to turn off Reels or remove the Reels tab.** Its own tools only change which Reels you see. To use Instagram on iPhone without Reels and Explore, but with your DMs, stories and friends' posts, use StillTabs: it's free and removes them in Standard mode.

## Is there a setting to turn off Reels?

**There is no off switch for Reels, the Reels tab or Explore in Instagram.** None of Instagram's help pages describe one, and people have petitioned for it for years. What Instagram offers instead are ways to shape the recommendations: they make Reels different, not gone.

## What Instagram's own settings can do

- **Not interested** on a recommended post or Reel shows you fewer like it.

- **Hide suggested posts** and filter suggested posts by words, emojis or hashtags in Settings → Suggested content.

- **Reset suggested content** clears recommendations across Explore, Reels and Feed. It starts over; it doesn't stop.

- **Your Algorithm**, an icon at the top of Reels, lets you add topics or ask for more or less of them.

- **Following and Favorites**: tap the Instagram logo to switch your feed to accounts you follow, in time order. The app still opens on the ranked feed.

- **Daily limit**: for adults it's a reminder, not a block.

All of these leave the Reels tab in the middle of the screen, one tap away.

## How to use Instagram without Reels on iPhone

1. Install [StillTabs](https://apps.apple.com/app/id6761367777) from the App Store. It's free.

2. Open StillTabs and tap the Instagram tile.

3. Sign in on Instagram's own login page, as you would in Safari.

4. Leave the mode on **Standard**: the Reels tab, Explore and suggested posts are gone, videos in your feed stop playing by themselves, and your feed shows only people you follow, newest first.

**Your DMs, stories and friends' posts stay exactly where they were.** Want no Reels at all, not even from friends? **Strict** removes every Reel in your feed and Explore and swaps the home feed for search while keeping stories. **Blackout** leaves only search, profiles and messages.

## Does it break Instagram or my account?

No. StillTabs loads Instagram's own website and only changes what is drawn on your screen, on your phone. You sign in on Instagram's page, StillTabs never sees your password, and nothing about your account changes. Turn the mode to Off and Instagram looks normal again.
