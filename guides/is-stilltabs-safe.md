# Is StillTabs safe?

*Updated 4 November 2026*

**Yes. StillTabs is a free App Store app that never sees your passwords or messages: you sign in on each platform's own page, and pages load straight from Instagram, YouTube and the rest, with no StillTabs server in between.** Its App Store label shows no tracking, analytics are off unless you switch them on, and there is nothing to buy.

## The facts

| Question | Answer |
|---|---|
| Is it on the App Store? | Yes, reviewed by Apple like every App Store app |
| Does it cost anything? | No. No subscription, no in-app purchases, no ads |

| Does it touch my other apps? | No. No Screen Time access, no device management, nothing locked |

## How it works

**StillTabs shows each platform's own mobile website and hides the parts built for endless scrolling**, the way a content blocker works in Safari. The filters run on your phone. Because nothing passes through a StillTabs server, there is nothing for us to store, leak or sell.

**StillTabs does not track you.** Its App Store privacy label is "Data Not Linked to You", with nothing under "Data Used to Track You" or "Data Linked to You". Usage analytics are off by default and only start if you switch them on; even then they are tied to a random ID on your phone, never to your name or accounts, and never include what you watch, search or write. There are no ads and nothing is sold. Pages load straight from each platform: StillTabs runs no proxy, so it never sees your passwords, messages or what you browse. The [privacy policy](https://stilltabs.com/privacy) lists every event in full.
