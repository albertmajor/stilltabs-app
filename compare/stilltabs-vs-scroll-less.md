# StillTabs vs Scroll Less: blocking Reels inside the Instagram app, or around it

*Updated 8 November 2026*

**Scroll Less blocks Reels and Shorts inside the official Instagram and YouTube apps by running an iOS screen broadcast; StillTabs is a free Scroll Less alternative that shows Instagram and YouTube in its own app with Reels, Shorts, Explore and suggested posts removed, and needs no broadcast.** StillTabs is free with nothing to buy and nothing running in the background; Scroll Less sells Premium from $4.99 to $49.99, and its in-app blocking only works while a screen broadcast keeps running.

## The short version

Both apps let you keep Instagram DMs, search and stories while cutting out the endless video feeds. They get there in opposite ways.

- **Scroll Less works inside the real apps.** You choose what to block, then start its Blocker. iOS asks you to start a broadcast for Scroll Less, and that is how it spots Reels and Shorts as they appear. Its website says detection runs on your iPhone and nothing on your screen is sent anywhere. You can stop the broadcast from Control Center.

- **StillTabs works around the real apps.** It opens Instagram and YouTube inside StillTabs, with Reels, Shorts, Explore, suggested posts, "For you" feeds and autoplay already gone. Home Screen icons named Instagram and YouTube open StillTabs, so your thumb goes where it always did. It never locks or changes the official apps.

Scroll Less also has a second mode, which it calls Hide: you open Instagram, YouTube or Reddit inside Scroll Less's built-in browser and hide things like Explore, the home feed, suggested posts and related videos. That part works much like StillTabs.

## Where StillTabs is better

- **Nothing to buy.** Every StillTabs feature, including the strictest mode, is free. Scroll Less is free to download but sells Premium, from $4.99 to $49.99. Its website sends you to the App Store page for current pricing, and we could not find a list of which features are paid.

- **Nothing has to keep running.** Scroll Less only blocks Reels in the official apps while its screen broadcast is on; stop it from Control Center and Reels are back until you tap Resume Blocker. StillTabs has no broadcast: Reels simply aren't there.

- **Problems reported by users.** The most recent review on Scroll Less's US App Store page (18 October 2026, one star) says it doesn't work, and an earlier reviewer couldn't get suggested-post blocking to work.

- **No permissions over your phone.** StillTabs doesn't need a screen broadcast, Screen Time access, device management or a Safari extension. It never locks or changes other apps. [What to check before giving any app these permissions](../guides/how-to-check-a-screen-time-app.md).

- **Explore and suggestions go too.** StillTabs removes Explore, suggested posts, "For you" feeds and autoplay in the same place it removes Reels and Shorts. Scroll Less's website and blog describe its official-app Blocker as covering Reels and Shorts, with Explore, suggested posts and the feeds hidden in its built-in browser.

- **Analytics are opt-in.** StillTabs keeps analytics off unless you switch them on. Pages load straight from each platform, so your passwords and messages never pass through StillTabs. [Is StillTabs safe?](../guides/is-stilltabs-safe.md)

- **Older iPhones.** StillTabs runs on iOS 16.0; Scroll Less needs iOS 17.6.

- **It feels like the app.** StillTabs draws Instagram's toolbar natively, keeps tabs loaded and supports edge swipes, with up to five accounts per platform. [What that looks like](../guides/does-stilltabs-feel-like-instagram.md).

## Where Scroll Less is better

## Can you block Reels inside the Instagram app on iPhone?

Only partly. Instagram has no switch to turn Reels off, and iOS doesn't let one app change another. Scroll Less gets around this by watching your screen through a broadcast and blocking Reels when it spots them, which only works while the broadcast is running.

StillTabs takes a different route: you can keep the official app installed but open Instagram through StillTabs instead, using a Home Screen icon named Instagram. ## Common questions

### What is a good Scroll Less alternative?

If you want something completely free with no screen broadcast, StillTabs shows Instagram and YouTube without Reels, Shorts, Explore or suggested posts and keeps your DMs. FeedOff and Nope are also free, with no in-app purchases listed. Scroll Less works inside the official apps, but only while its screen broadcast runs, and it sells Premium.

### Is Scroll Less free?

It is free to download and sells in-app purchases on the US App Store: Scroll Less Premium $4.99, Premium Annual $29.99, Lifetime $14.99 and $49.99, and a $5.99 lifetime offer. We could not find which features need Premium. StillTabs has no in-app purchases.

### How does Scroll Less block Reels inside the Instagram app?

You start its Blocker, and iOS asks you to start a screen broadcast for Scroll Less. Its website says that is how it detects Reels and Shorts, on your iPhone, without sending your screen anywhere. You can stop it from Control Center.

### Does StillTabs work inside the official Instagram app?

No. StillTabs shows Instagram inside its own app with Reels, Explore and suggested posts removed. A Home Screen icon named Instagram opens StillTabs, so you don't need to open the official app. It never locks or changes other apps.
