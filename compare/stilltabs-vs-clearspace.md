# StillTabs vs Clearspace

*Updated 4 November 2026*

**Clearspace makes you do something (like push-ups) before opening Instagram; StillTabs removes the Reels so there's nothing to resist.** StillTabs is free with no tracking. Clearspace costs $6.99 a month or up to $59.99 a year, and its App Store label lists tracking plus Health & Fitness and Contacts as data linked to you.

## Side by side

|   | StillTabs | Clearspace |
|---|---|---|
| What it does | Removes Reels, Shorts and feeds inside the platform | Adds a task or delay before opening whole apps |
| After you open Instagram | No Reels tab, no Explore, Following feed | Normal Instagram, Reels included |
| Price | Free | $6.99 a month, $44.99 to $59.99 a year, Family $79.99 |
| Data Used to Track You | None | Contact Info, Usage Data |
| Data Linked to You | None | Health & Fitness, Contact Info, Contacts, Identifiers, Usage Data, Diagnostics, Other Data |
| Its own terms | Short, plain terms | None of its own; Apple's standard licence |

## What to check before you subscribe

- **Health data.** The App Store label lists Health & Fitness as linked to you, but its privacy policy (updated 27 May 2026) never mentions health or fitness.

- **Location.** The policy allows it to "track location-based information from your mobile device, either continuously or while you are using our App".

- **Paywall.** 26 of its 87 recent 1–2 star reviews mention payment, some about having to pay to block more than one app.

Review counts: the most recent written reviews in Apple's public US review feed (up to 500 per app), 1–2 star reviews sorted by topic on 4 November 2026. They are what users wrote, not verified facts.
