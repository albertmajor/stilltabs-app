# Can iPhone Screen Time block Reels or Shorts?

*Updated 4 November 2026*

**No. iPhone Screen Time can limit or block whole apps, but it can't remove Reels from inside Instagram or Shorts from inside YouTube.** A Screen Time limit on Instagram also locks your messages. StillTabs removes just Reels and Shorts and keeps the rest, for free.

## What Screen Time does

**Screen Time works on whole apps and categories.** On iOS 26 that means Downtime and App Limits; iOS 27 replaces them with Screen Time Schedules and Time Allowances. Either way, the smallest thing it can limit is an app (or, in Safari, a whole website). It can't see inside Instagram to tell Reels from your messages, so limiting Instagram limits all of it.

Limits can also be ignored with a tap unless you set a Screen Time passcode and turn on blocking, which is why many people find them easy to wave away.
