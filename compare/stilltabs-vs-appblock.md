# StillTabs vs AppBlock

*Updated 4 November 2026*

**AppBlock's own help page says it "cannot block specific features or sections within an app (like Reels, Shorts, or Stories)". StillTabs does exactly that, for free.** AppBlock Premium is up to $14.99 a month, and its App Store label lists Identifiers as data used to track you.

## Side by side

|   | StillTabs | AppBlock |
|---|---|---|
| Block only Reels or Shorts | Yes | No, by its own help page |
| Price | Free | $4.99–$14.99/month, $29.99–$59.99/year |
| Data Used to Track You | None | Identifiers |
| Ad and measurement SDKs in its policy | None | Meta SDK, Singular, RevenueCat, Superwall, Firebase |

## What to check before you subscribe

- **"Never shared", but processors listed.** The App Store description says "Your personal data stays private and is never shared." Its privacy policy lists the Facebook (Meta) SDK and Singular, "a mobile measurement partner … used to analyze and attribute user acquisition and marketing campaign performance".

- **Refund requests.** Its terms: "you consent to our sharing of data regarding your usage and consumption of purchased content with Apple, as part of our efforts to resolve refund requests."

- **Trials convert automatically:** "unless you cancelled your Subscription, you will be automatically charged".

- **Reviews:** 122 of its 350 available US reviews are 1–2 stars; 58 of those mention payment.

Review counts: the most recent written reviews in Apple's public US review feed (up to 500 per app), 1–2 star reviews sorted by topic on 4 November 2026. They are what users wrote, not verified facts.
