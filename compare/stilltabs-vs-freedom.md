## What to check before you subscribe

- **Its own help page on bypassing:** "Normally, you can go to Settings > Screen Time and turn off Freedom's screen time permissions to get around blocks — even during an active block session." The fix needs iOS 26.4 or later.

- **A 50-item limit:** "Apple's Screen Time limits the number of apps and categories that can be blocked during any given block session (up to 50)."

- **Terms can change without notice**, and we found no refund policy page.

- **Its privacy policy** says "We use the Meta Pixel to market our product through Meta platforms."

- **Payment is the top complaint:** 67 of the 126 1–2 star reviews among its 500 most recent US reviews.

Review counts: the most recent written reviews in Apple's public US review feed (up to 500 per app), 1–2 star reviews sorted by topic on 4 November 2026. They are what users wrote, not verified facts.
