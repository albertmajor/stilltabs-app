# StillTabs vs Opal: removing Reels vs blocking the whole app

*Updated 4 November 2026*

**Opal blocks whole apps; StillTabs removes only the addictive parts.** With Opal, Instagram is either locked or fully open, Reels included. With StillTabs you keep Instagram for messages and friends, minus Reels and Explore, for free. Opal's plans run from $4.99 a week to $99.99 a year, and its App Store label lists Identifiers as data used to track you.

## The real difference

Blocking works when you want to stay out of an app completely. But most people don't want to quit Instagram; they want to answer a message without being pulled into Reels. **A blocker can't do that, because Reels live inside the same app as your DMs.** StillTabs takes the Reels out and leaves the messages.

## What to check before you subscribe

- **Weekly billing.** The App Store lists a "Weekly Plan" at $4.99 and $9.99, a student weekly plan at $9.99, $19.99 a month and $99.99 a year.

- **No refunds through the App Store.** Its terms: for App Store and Google Play purchases "we are unable to provide refunds".

- **"Can't bypass", with caveats.** The App Store description says "Deep Focus won't let you cancel or bypass at all". Opal's own help page says that before iOS 26.4, "Face ID or your device passcode can be inputted to bypass it."

- **Label vs policy.** Opal's policy summary lists Usage Data as "not linked to you"; the App Store label lists it as linked, and adds "Data Used to Track You: Identifiers". The policy also covers the case "to the extent Opal sells your Personal Data" under California law.

- **Recent reviews.** Of Opal's 500 most recent US reviews (13 July to 2 November 2026), **281 were 1 or 2 stars**, against a lifetime average of 4.7.

Review counts: the most recent written reviews in Apple's public US review feed (up to 500 per app), 1–2 star reviews sorted by topic on 4 November 2026. They are what users wrote, not verified facts.
