# StillTabs vs Jomo

*Updated 4 November 2026*

**Jomo blocks whole apps on a schedule; StillTabs removes the Reels and keeps the rest of Instagram.** StillTabs is free with no tracking. Jomo Plus is $5.99 a month, $29.99 a year or up to $99.99 lifetime, and its App Store label lists User Content as data used to track you.

|   | StillTabs | Jomo |
|---|---|---|
| What it does | Removes Reels, Shorts and feeds | Blocks whole apps and websites |
| Keep Instagram DMs, lose Reels | Yes | No |

## What to check before you subscribe

- **Its terms let it end your account** "immediately, without prior notice or liability, under our sole discretion, for any reason whatsoever", lifetime plan or not.

- **Its policy names Facebook and OpenAI** among the third parties it uses.

- Fairly, Jomo's reviews are mostly positive, and its recent reviews show few complaints.
