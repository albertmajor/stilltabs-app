# StillTabs vs one sec

*Updated 4 November 2026*

**one sec makes you pause before opening Instagram; StillTabs removes Reels once you're inside.** A pause helps with opening an app on autopilot, but once you're in, Reels are all there. StillTabs takes them out and keeps your DMs, for free. one sec pro is listed at $6.99 a month, $19.99 a year or $99.99 lifetime.

## Side by side

|   | StillTabs | one sec |
|---|---|---|
| What it does | Removes Reels, Shorts, Explore and "For you" inside the platform | Adds a breathing pause before opening chosen apps |
| After you open Instagram | No Reels, no Explore | Normal Instagram, Reels included |
| Price | Free, no in-app purchases | Free download; pro $6.99 a month, $19.99 a year, $99.99 lifetime |
| Setup | Install and open | Shortcuts automation per app |

## What to check before you subscribe

- **It's paid for the full product**: pro is $6.99 a month, $19.99 a year or $99.99 lifetime.

- **Easy to switch off.** one sec's own blog says that only "with iOS 26.4, you can finally lock the screen time permission for one sec with a code", so on earlier iOS versions the permission couldn't be locked.

- **It never removes Reels.** Once the pause is over, Instagram opens as normal.

- **What reviewers mention most.** Of the 123 1–2 star reviews among its 500 most recent US reviews (March 2024 to November 2026), 60 mention payment.

To be fair, one sec's privacy practices are clean: its App Store label shows no tracking. Its problem is the approach, not the data.

Review counts: the most recent written reviews in Apple's public US review feed (up to 500 per app), 1–2 star reviews sorted by topic on 4 November 2026. They are what users wrote, not verified facts.

## Which one fits

If your problem is opening apps without meaning to, a pause helps. **If your problem is what happens after you open them** (one message turns into forty minutes of Reels), you need the Reels gone, and that's what StillTabs does.
