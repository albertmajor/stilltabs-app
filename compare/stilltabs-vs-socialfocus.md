# StillTabs vs SocialFocus

*Updated 4 November 2026*

**SocialFocus is a Safari extension that costs $4.99 to download and then sells subscriptions up to $79.99; StillTabs is a free app.** SocialFocus's own listing says it "does NOT work with the apps, it works only for websites while using the Safari browser."

## Side by side

|   | StillTabs | SocialFocus |
|---|---|---|
| Price | Free | $4.99 upfront, plus $3.99/month, $35.99/year or $79.99 lifetime |
| Where it works | Its own app, one home for five platforms | Safari only |
| Last update | Updated regularly (46 filter changes since March 2026) | 2 July 2026 |
| App Store rating | New | 3.95 from 327; 59 are 1 star |

## What to check before you subscribe

- **Its terms** reserve the right "to charge for its services, at any time and for any reason" and to "terminate use of it at any time without giving notice".

- **Reviews:** of 44 1–2 star US reviews, 29 say it stopped working or is buggy, and 5 complain about paying upfront and then being asked to subscribe.

Review counts: the most recent written reviews in Apple's public US review feed (up to 500 per app), 1–2 star reviews sorted by topic on 4 November 2026. They are what users wrote, not verified facts.
