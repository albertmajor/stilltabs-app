# Is SocialLite free? What you get without Pro

*Updated 8 November 2026*

**SocialLite is free to download, and the free tier removes Reels, Shorts and Explore, but you can't change its blocking settings without a paid Pro or Family plan.** SocialLite's terms list Pro at $6.99 a month. The US App Store lists ten in-app purchases from $3.99 to $47.90. If you want every setting free, StillTabs, FeedOff and Nope have no in-app purchases at all.

## What the free version of SocialLite includes

SocialLite's terms of service (last updated 18 February 2026) describe three tiers:

- **Free:** "Access to all 7 supported platforms with default content blocking. Content blocking settings are not customizable."

- **Pro, $6.99 a month:** "Full customization of content blocking settings and additional features."

- **Family, $13.99 a month:** "Everything in Pro, plus parental controls for managing child accounts."

Reels, Shorts and Explore are blocked on every plan, including Free. The terms call these "core locks" that can't be turned off on any plan. So the free version does the main job. What you pay for is control: Pro lets you switch individual blocks on or off. The FAQ also says that "SocialLite subscribers" can add up to 10 Instagram accounts.

The terms list seven platforms: Instagram, YouTube, X, TikTok, Facebook, LinkedIn and Snapchat. The App Store update notes for version 3.1.0 add Reddit. SocialLite's iPhone FAQ says TikTok, Snapchat, LinkedIn, X and Facebook are still in beta.

The US App Store page lists these in-app purchases, in this order:

- SocialLite Pro Yearly: $29.99

- SocialLite Pro Yearly: $47.90

- SocialLite Pro Monthly: $6.99

- SocialLite Pro Yearly: $47.90

- Sociallite Pro: $3.99

- SocialLite Family Monthly: $12.99

- SocialLite Pro Monthly: $17.99

So there are several prices for the same plan name: Pro Monthly at $6.99, $9.99 or $17.99, and Pro Yearly at $19.99, $29.99 or $47.90. The App Store doesn't say which offer you will see. The listing shows Family Monthly at $12.99, while the terms say $13.99. SocialLite's FAQ says the price and billing period are "shown before you confirm the purchase", and that a free trial "is not guaranteed". Check the price on the purchase sheet before you tap.

From the terms: subscriptions renew automatically unless you cancel, cancelling takes effect at the end of the billing period, there are "no partial refunds", and SocialLite "generally" does not refund subscription payments.

## What SocialLite's privacy label says

Apple's label for SocialLite has a **"Data Used to Track You"** section listing *Identifiers*. That means data that may be used to track you across apps and websites owned by other companies. Under "Data Not Linked to You" it lists:

- **Developer's Advertising or Marketing:** purchase history, user ID, product interaction, advertising data.

- **App Functionality:** purchase history, email address, name, phone number, user ID, device ID, product interaction.

The App Store description says the only data used is opt-in, "to see which ads you saw to use SocialLite". SocialLite's FAQ says it doesn't collect, store or sell data about your social media use, and that you log in directly with each platform so it never sees your password. Its privacy policy says it does not read your DMs.

StillTabs' label is "Data Not Linked to You", with no tracking section. Analytics stay off unless you turn them on, and stilltabs.com loads no third-party trackers. More in [Is StillTabs safe?](../guides/is-stilltabs-safe.md)

## Free alternatives to SocialLite

- **StillTabs** (free, no in-app purchases). Shows Instagram and YouTube inside the app without Reels, Shorts, Explore, suggested posts, "For you" feeds or autoplay. DMs, stories, search, subscriptions and posts from people you follow stay. All four modes are free, including Blackout, which leaves only search, profiles and DMs. Facebook, X and Reddit are in beta. It needs no account and no Screen Time permission. [More on StillTabs as a SocialLite alternative](sociallite-alternative.md).

- **FeedOff** (free, no in-app purchases). A browser for Instagram, YouTube, X, Reddit, LinkedIn and Facebook with Reels, Shorts, Explore, For You and suggested posts hidden. Each hide is a toggle. It can also block the real apps on a schedule using Apple's Screen Time. Its privacy label is "Data Not Collected".

- **Nope** (free, no in-app purchases). A Safari extension that hides Reels, Shorts, Explore and more when you use instagram.com and youtube.com in Safari. It has 17 toggles and no account. Its label is also "Data Not Collected". It works on the websites in Safari, not inside the Instagram or YouTube apps.

None of these covers TikTok or Snapchat inside the app. For more options, see [free apps that block Reels](../guides/free-app-to-block-reels.md).

## Is SocialLite Pro worth it?

It depends on what you need. If the default blocks suit you, the free tier already removes Reels, Shorts and Explore, and you don't need Pro. Pro makes sense if you want to fine-tune individual blocks, use several Instagram accounts in SocialLite, or set up a Family plan for a child.

## Common questions

### Is SocialLite free?

The download is free and the free tier blocks Reels, Shorts and Explore by default. Changing the blocking settings needs a paid Pro or Family plan, according to SocialLite's terms.

### How much is SocialLite Pro?

SocialLite's terms say $6.99 a month. The US App Store lists several Pro prices: monthly at $6.99, $9.99 or $17.99, yearly at $19.99, $29.99 or $47.90, and one "Sociallite Pro" item at $3.99. The price is shown before you confirm.

### Does SocialLite have a free trial?

SocialLite's FAQ says a free trial "is not guaranteed" and depends on your eligibility, plan, platform and country. If you qualify, the trial length and the price after it are shown before you buy.

### Can I get a refund for SocialLite Pro?

SocialLite's terms say it "generally" does not refund subscription payments and gives no partial refunds, with exceptions at its discretion for billing errors or technical issues. Purchases go through Apple, so Apple's own refund request process also applies.

### What is a free alternative to SocialLite?

StillTabs, FeedOff and Nope are free with no in-app purchases. StillTabs and FeedOff show Instagram and YouTube in their own app without Reels and Shorts; Nope does it in Safari.
