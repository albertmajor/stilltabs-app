# StillTabs vs Stillfeed

*Updated 4 November 2026*

**StillTabs and Stillfeed are different apps from different makers.** StillTabs is free, with no in-app purchases and nothing linked to you. Stillfeed bills $2.99 a week (about $155 a year), and its App Store label lists Browsing History and Coarse Location as data linked to you.

## Side by side

|   | StillTabs | Stillfeed |
|---|---|---|
| Price | Free | $2.99/week, $5.99/month, $44.99/year |
| Data Linked to You | None | Browsing History, Coarse Location, Purchase History, User ID, Device ID, Advertising Data and more |
| App Store age rating, under "Contains" | No ads | "Advertising" |
| Analytics | Off until you turn them on | On by default, per its policy |
| App Store ratings | New, out since September 2026 | No ratings |

## What to check before you subscribe

- **On by default.** Its policy: analytics, crash reports and ad-partner signals "are on by default", and the ad token "is normally sent the first time you open Stillfeed, before you can reach Settings".

- **Unnamed ad partners.** "A list of these partners is available on request."

- **Screen recordings** of its own screens are kept 30 days.

- **Price testing:** "We may show different subscription prices to different users".

- **"Anonymous" vs "Linked".** The policy calls the data anonymous; the App Store label declares it as linked to you.
