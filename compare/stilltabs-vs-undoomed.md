*Updated 8 November 2026*

## Is UNDOOMED free?

Partly, and the free tier is generous. Free gets you all seven networks with one account each, the core filters (Reels, Explore, Shorts and similar), hourly and daily time limits, the 4-digit Parent Lock and a 7-day usage overview. No account or card is needed.

The paid plans add the rest. On the US App Store, Plus costs $1.99 a month or $11.99 a year and adds detailed statistics, colour accents and a second account per network. Pro costs $2.99 a month, $17.99 a year or $29.99 once, and adds 52 advanced filters, including Messages Only, Hide Stories and Hide Sponsored Posts, plus up to five accounts per network. UNDOOMED's website says eligible monthly plans on iOS may include a 3-day trial.

StillTabs has no paid tier. Its four modes and up to five accounts per platform are free for everyone. See [what other Reels blockers charge](hidden-costs-of-reels-blockers.md).

## Where StillTabs is better

- **Nothing to unlock.** Every StillTabs feature is free. In UNDOOMED, a DMs-only view, hiding Stories and more than one account per network need a paid plan.

- **A DMs-only mode for free.** StillTabs' Blackout mode leaves only search, profiles and messages. [How to use Instagram only for messages](../guides/instagram-only-for-messages.md).

- **Five accounts at no cost.** StillTabs lets you sign in to up to five accounts per platform. UNDOOMED allows one free, five with Pro. [Multiple accounts without Reels](../guides/multiple-accounts-without-reels.md).

- **Your old habit still works.** StillTabs sets up Home Screen icons named Instagram, YouTube and so on that open StillTabs, so your thumb goes where it always did. No Screen Time permission. [How the icons work](../guides/instagram-icon-without-reels.md).

- **It feels like the app.** StillTabs draws Instagram's toolbar natively, keeps each tab loaded and supports edge swipes. [What that looks like](../guides/does-stilltabs-feel-like-instagram.md).

- **A slightly older iOS works.** StillTabs needs iOS 16.0; UNDOOMED needs 16.4.

## Where UNDOOMED is better

- **More networks.** UNDOOMED covers Threads and LinkedIn, which StillTabs doesn't, and treats Facebook, X and Reddit as full networks rather than beta.

- **Time limits and a PIN lock, free.** If you want a daily cap, or a parent wants settings protected, UNDOOMED has both in its free tier. StillTabs has neither.

- **Usage numbers.** UNDOOMED shows how much you scroll, with a 7-day overview free and longer graphs with Plus. StillTabs shows no stats.

- **Android, iPad and 20 languages.** StillTabs is iPhone only and in English for now.

- **A track record.** UNDOOMED has been on the App Store since October 2025, has 108 US ratings averaging 4.3 and was last updated on 22 October 2026. StillTabs is new, with no ratings yet.

## What they have in common

Both apps show each network's mobile website inside their own app and filter what that page shows. Neither modifies the official apps, and neither needs an account. Both keep analytics off until you agree to them, and both App Store labels say "Data Not Linked to You".

Neither covers TikTok or Snapchat. UNDOOMED lists both but marks them unavailable, saying their web versions expose too little to filter usefully; StillTabs doesn't support them either.

## Which one should you pick?

- **You mainly use Instagram and YouTube and want it all free:** StillTabs.

- **You want Instagram for DMs only without paying:** StillTabs' Blackout mode.

- **You need Threads, LinkedIn, Android, time limits or a parental PIN:** UNDOOMED.

- **You're not sure:** both are free to try. Install one, use it for a week, then decide.

## Common questions

### Is UNDOOMED free?

It has a free tier with core filters (Reels, Shorts, Explore and similar) on seven networks and includes time limits and a PIN lock. Messages Only mode, Hide Stories, 52 advanced filters and extra accounts need Plus ($1.99 a month or $11.99 a year) or Pro ($2.99 a month, $17.99 a year or $29.99 lifetime) on the US App Store. StillTabs has no paid tier.

### What is a free alternative to UNDOOMED?

StillTabs is completely free, with no in-app purchases, and includes a DMs-only mode and up to five accounts per platform. It covers Instagram and YouTube, with Facebook, X and Reddit in beta.

### UNDOOMED vs StillTabs: which is better for Instagram DMs only?

Both keep DMs. StillTabs' Blackout mode, which leaves only search, profiles and DMs, is free. UNDOOMED's Messages Only mode is part of Pro.

### Does StillTabs support Threads and LinkedIn like UNDOOMED?

No. StillTabs covers Instagram and YouTube, with Facebook, X and Reddit in beta. If you need Threads or LinkedIn, UNDOOMED covers them. Neither app supports TikTok or Snapchat.

### Do UNDOOMED and StillTabs collect data?

Both App Store labels say "Data Not Linked to You", and both keep analytics off until you agree. UNDOOMED's label lists Device ID, Product Interaction and Crash Data. StillTabs loads pages straight from each platform, so passwords and messages never pass through StillTabs.
