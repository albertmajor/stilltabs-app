# StillTabs vs ScrollGuard

*Updated 4 November 2026*

**ScrollGuard is a Safari extension for social websites; StillTabs is a free app that gives Instagram, YouTube, Facebook, X and Reddit a calm home without Reels or Shorts.** ScrollGuard's free version is limited to "a limited number of filters", with Pro from $3.99 to $59.99.

## Side by side

|   | StillTabs | ScrollGuard |
|---|---|---|
| Where it works | Its own app | Safari, on the websites |
| Price | Free, everything included | Free version with "a limited number of filters"; Pro $3.99 to $59.99 |
| Separate accounts per platform | Up to five, isolated | Whatever Safari is signed in to |
| Launcher, screen-time tiles, widget | Yes | No |

## What to check before you subscribe

- **What it says it is.** Its llms.txt says it removes short-form video "from social media apps"; the App Store description says it "uses a Safari browser extension to hide the most addictive parts of social media websites".

- **Analytics.** Its llms.txt says it "does not collect usage analytics"; its privacy policy says it uses "Sentry, Firebase, or similar services" to collect anonymous usage data.

- **Its terms:** "Deleting the app does not cancel your subscription."
