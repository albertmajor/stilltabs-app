## The four ways to block Reels on iPhone

**iPhone apps can't change how another app looks**, so every Reels blocker uses one of four workarounds. The kind matters more than the brand:

- **Its own app, with the platforms inside** (StillTabs, FeedOff, minulo, UNDOOMED, SocialLite, Dull). Reels are removed before you see them and messages keep working. StillTabs has no paid tier at all.

- **Safari extensions** (Nope, Slowth, #blockit, ScrollGuard). They work on the websites in Safari, and you switch them on per site in Settings (Nope's website says its app can also open Instagram and YouTube itself). Fine if you already use Instagram in Safari.

- **Screen broadcast** (Scroll Less). iOS asks you to start a broadcast, the same system feature used for screen recording, so the app can detect Reels on your screen inside the official apps. The broadcast has to keep running; Scroll Less says detection stays on your phone. It also has a separate mode that opens Instagram, YouTube and Reddit inside its own app, like the apps above.

- **Whole-app blockers** (Opal, one sec, ScreenZen, Screen Time). They lock or delay Instagram as a whole, so your messages are locked along with the Reels.
