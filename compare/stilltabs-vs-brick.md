# StillTabs vs Brick

*Updated 4 November 2026*

**Brick locks whole apps until you tap a $59 device (or the $129 Brick Night); StillTabs removes just Reels and Shorts, for free, with nothing to carry or lose.** Locked Instagram means locked DMs; with StillTabs your messages stay.

## Side by side

|   | StillTabs | Brick |
|---|---|---|
| Cost | Free | $59 device; Brick Night $129 |
| Instagram DMs while focused | Yes | Only by unbricking, Reels included |
| If you lose it | Nothing to lose | 5 Emergency Unbricks, then a form and up to 2 business days |
| Needs | iOS 16 | iOS 18 |

## What to check before you buy

- **Losing the device:** "You get 5 [Emergency Unbricks] … If you've used all your Emergency Unbricks and need them reset, please fill out this form and we will reset within 2 business days."

- **Terms:** it can "change Fees at any time", and disputes go to arbitration in Madison, Wisconsin.

- **Marketing data:** its privacy policy says it uses "consumer profiles that contain data from other sites or from other third parties for advertising purposes", and retargets through Meta, Google and TikTok ads.
